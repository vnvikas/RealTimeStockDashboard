About {{ app_name }}

Your complete stock market tracking and portfolio management solution

Our Mission

To empower investors of all levels with real-time market data, powerful analytical tools, and intuitive portfolio management capabilities. We believe that everyone should have access to the information they need to make informed investment decisions.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and comprehensive stock market dashboard platform, bridging the gap between complex financial data and everyday investors through innovative technology and user-centered design.

Key Features

Real-Time Data

Live stock prices, market indices, and crypto data with automatic updates every 30 seconds.

Portfolio Management

Track your investments, calculate profit/loss, and analyze performance with interactive charts.

Price Alerts

Get notified when stocks reach your target prices or when important market events occur.

Interactive Charts

Visualize market data with line charts, candlestick charts, and volume analysis using Plotly.

Market News

Stay informed with the latest financial news, stock-specific updates, and market analysis.

Security

Secure authentication, password hashing, CSRF protection, and XSS prevention for your safety.
